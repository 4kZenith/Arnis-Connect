# Arnis Connect tester guide

Use this while you try the live site: https://arnis-connect.great-site.net/

Arnis Connect is the club’s record of people, practice, attendance, events, and private-session payments. Every person has one login and, for players and coaches, one member profile. The email on those two screens is the same email. Times on the site are Philippine time.

Test with your own name and a real email address you can open. Do not share one login between two people. Write down anything that does not match the “You should see” line: your role, the page, what you clicked, and the time.

There is no monthly membership due. Public training is the group session. Private training is a separate paid session.

## Who should test

Ask the Admin to prepare these accounts before the group starts. Each person keeps their own username and password.

| Person | How the account is made | What they are for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admin | Already exists | Creates staff, approves players, and checks the whole club |
| Head Coach | Admin → Users → Add New User | Sees every member and can edit them |
| Coach | Admin → Users → Add New User | Handles their own sessions and the players on those sessions |
| Finance | Admin → Users → Add New User, role Finance | Records money and approves private-session receipts |
| Public player | Register as Member, category Public Player | Group training and face check-in |
| Private player | Register as Member, category Private Player | Group training plus paid private sessions |
| Mixed player | Register as Member, category Mixed / Both | Both kinds of training |

Admin, Head Coach, Coach, and Finance accounts are created by the Admin. Players sign up themselves and wait for approval.

## Before the group starts (Admin)

1. Sign in. Open **Settings**.
2. Confirm email sending is turned on, and that a test message can leave the club’s email.
3. Leave check-in opening at 30 minutes before the session, and the late grace period at 15 minutes, unless the group needs a wider window to practice check-in.
4. Leave the private session fee at ₱500 unless the club wants a different standard fee.
5. Create the Head Coach, Coach, and Finance logins. Use each person’s real email. Their member profile is created with that same email.
6. Create one training for today. Set the start time so it is either happening now or starts within 30 minutes. Assign the test Coach as the coach. Choose a place the phones can locate.
7. After the players are approved, add the public, private, and mixed players to that training.

Account emails (the verification link and the password reset) are sent even when ordinary notices are off. Training, event, rank, and payment emails need the notice switch on.

## How to read a result

- **Pass:** the screen matches the “You should see” line.
- **Blocked, and that is correct:** you are not allowed to do that action. Note it as a pass for this role.
- **Fail:** you were allowed to do something this role should not do, or a step you should complete shows an error.

## 1. New player (Applicant)

You do not have an account yet. You are checking signup.

1. Open the site. Choose **Register as Member**.
2. Enter your real name, birthdate, email, mobile number, and a username. Password must be at least 6 characters.
3. Choose **Public Player**, **Private Player**, or **Mixed / Both**. Beginners can leave rank at None.
4. Submit the form.
5. Open your email and use the verification link. Check the spam folder if it is not in the inbox.
6. Try to sign in before you open that link.

You should see: sign-in is refused until the email is verified.

7. After the link works, sign in with the username and password you chose.

You should see: a small dashboard only. You are waiting for the Admin to approve you. You do not get Members, Trainings, Funds, Users, Reports, or Settings.

8. Wait for the Admin to approve you, then sign out and sign in again.

You should see: you are now a Member, and **My Profile** appears.

Also try **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page with the same email. A reset message should arrive.

## 2. Member

Sign in after the Admin has approved you. The left menu is:

Dashboard, My Profile, Trainings, Private Training, Attendance Report, Events, Announcements.

Your category changes a few steps. Public players join group sessions. Private and Mixed players can also request a private session and pay for it.

### Profile and email

1. Open **My Profile**.
2. Change your mobile number and save.
3. Change the email to another address you can open, and save.
4. Ask the Admin to open your user account. You do not have a Users page.

You should see: the phone number saves. The Admin sees that same email on your login and on your member profile. Your name, birthdate, rank, and category stay as they were. Members do not change their own rank from the edit form.

### Face, once

1. On **My Profile**, enroll your face with the camera. Allow the camera.
2. When it saves, that photo and face are what check-in will compare.
3. Try to enroll a second time.

You should see: the second capture does not replace the face. It becomes a request. A Head Coach or the Admin compares the new photo with the one on file and approves or declines it. You can cancel your own pending request. A coach cannot approve a face change for you.

### Group training and check-in

1. Open **Trainings**. Open the session the Admin added you to.
2. If the session has not opened yet, the page tells you when check-in opens.
3. When it is open, choose **Check In: Face + GPS**. Allow the camera and location. Use your own face.
4. Look at your status afterward.

You should see:

- Check-in opens 30 minutes before the start and closes when the session ends.
- Checking in inside the first 15 minutes after the start is Present.
- Checking in after that grace period is Late.
- If you never check in, you stay Absent.
- You cannot mark anyone else’s attendance.
- You cannot create or edit the training.

### Rank request

1. On **My Profile**, request the next belt only.
2. Add a short note, a certificate photo, or both.
3. Submit, then wait.

You should see: the request is waiting. You cannot skip a belt. When a Coach, Head Coach, or Admin approves it, your rank moves up one step and you get an email. If they decline it, your rank stays and you get an email.

### Events and announcements

1. Open **Events**. You can read events. You cannot create one.
2. Open **Announcements**. Read the ones posted for everyone or for members.

You should see: those pages open. There is no button for you to post an announcement or add an event. If someone adds you to a session or event, or posts an announcement for members, the email arrives when notices are on.

### Private training

Public players: open **Private Training**. You should see the page, and you should not be able to submit a new paid request.

Private and Mixed players:

1. Open **Private Training** and submit a request. Pick a date from today onward, a coach, and a note.
2. Wait until a coach approves it.
3. If they approve it at the standard fee (₱500 unless Settings says otherwise), the session is confirmed. You should get an email.
4. If they approve it at a different fee, the request stays on **Awaiting Member**. Choose **Accept** or **Decline**.
5. Decline once, with a test request, so the coach can confirm they received “Private training offer declined”.
6. On a confirmed request, upload a photo of a GCash, Maya, bank, or other receipt. Choose the method that matches the photo.
7. Wait for Finance or the Admin to approve it.

You should see: after approval the session is paid. If they reject the photo, you can send another one. You cannot approve your own payment.

### What a member should be blocked from

Users, Funds, Reports, Activity Log, Settings, the full member directory, and deleting anyone. Opening those by guessing the page address should stop you.

## 3. Coach

The Admin creates this account with role Coach. Sign in. The menu adds **Members** to the member menu. You still do not get Funds, Users, Reports, Activity Log, or Settings.

Your member list is only the players who are already on a training you coach. Until the Admin puts players on your session, that list can be empty. That is expected.

### Your session

1. Open **Trainings**. Find the session where you are the coach.
2. Confirm the roster includes the test players.
3. After a player checks in, open the attendance list.

You should see: their status is Present or Late, with the face check-in. You can flag a check-in that looks wrong. You cannot edit a training that belongs to another coach.

### Your players

1. Open **Members**. Open one player from your session.
2. You can update ordinary profile details. Rank on the edit form stays put. Rank moves when you approve their promotion request, and only by one belt.
3. Approve one test player’s belt request. Decline a second one if a player submitted two.

You should see: the approved player’s rank is the next belt, and both players get an email. You cannot open a player who has never been on your session.

### Private request

1. Open **Private Training**.
2. Approve a private player’s request at ₱500. It becomes confirmed immediately.
3. Approve another request at a different amount, such as ₱600.

You should see: the ₱600 request waits for the player. If that player presses Decline, you get an email. You cannot approve the receipt photo. That belongs to Finance or the Admin.

### Face and announcements

You should see: a player’s second face enrollment stays a request. You cannot approve it. You can read announcements for everyone and for coaches. You cannot post one.

## 4. Head Coach

The Admin creates this account with role Head Coach. You can see and edit every member. You still do not get Funds, Users, Reports, Activity Log, or Settings, and you cannot delete a member.

1. Open **Members**. You should see the whole club, not only your own sessions.
2. Edit a member’s phone or email and save. Open that person’s login with the Admin later: the email matches.
3. You may correct a rank from the member edit screen when the club needs an exception. Players themselves still request one belt at a time.
4. Open the face-change requests. Compare the new photo with the face already on file. Approve one and decline one. You cannot approve your own request.
5. Open **Trainings** and **Events**. You can see all of them. Create or edit the ones you are responsible for.
6. Open **Announcements**. Post one for Members, with “Also send this by email” checked.

You should see: members receive that email. Coaches do not, unless you chose Everyone or Coaches. You cannot approve a private-session receipt and you cannot delete a person.

---

## 5. Finance

The Admin creates this account with role Finance. The menu is Dashboard, Members, Trainings, Private Training, Events, Funds, Announcements, and Reports. You can look at people and sessions. You do not change them, take attendance, or manage logins.

1. Open **Members** and **Trainings**. You can read them. Save is not available.
2. Open **Private Training**. Find a confirmed request with a receipt photo.
3. Approve one receipt.

You should see: the session is marked paid, and **Funds** has a matching income entry.

4. Reject a different receipt, or ask a player to send a test you can reject.

You should see: the player is asked for another photo. The session is not paid.

5. If a player paid in cash and no receipt is waiting, use **Mark Paid**.
6. Open **Funds** and add a normal expense, then confirm it appears.
7. Open **Reports** and confirm the money and attendance figures include what you just recorded.
8. Open **Announcements**. You see posts for everyone and for officers. You cannot post one.

You should see: no Users, Activity Log, or Settings. You cannot delete a member or change a rank.

---

## 6. Admin

You are the only role that creates logins, deletes people, and cleans the activity list. Do this last, after the other roles have produced real records.

### Accounts

1. Open **Users → Add New User**.
2. Add a Coach with a new email and leave Linked Member ID blank.
3. Open **Members** and find the profile that was created with that coach. The email matches.
4. Edit the email on the member screen and save. Open the user again.

You should see: both emails changed together. The same happens if you edit the user and save. If the login was created earlier and the member ID is blank, saving either screen still joins them when only one member has that name.

5. Approve the waiting applicant from the registration list.

You should see: they can sign in as a Member, and they receive a decision email.

6. Try **Forgot password** on a test account you own.

### Club records

1. Open **Settings** and confirm the check-in window, the late grace period, and the private fee are the numbers the group used.
2. Open **Trainings**, **Events**, and **Announcements** and confirm the records the other roles created are visible.
3. Approve or decline any face request and rank request that is still waiting. You cannot approve your own face request.
4. Open **Funds** and **Reports**. The approved private receipt is income.
5. Open **Activity Log** and the recent activity on the dashboard. Delete one line.

You should see: the line disappears. The training, payment, or member change it described is still there. Deleting the line does not undo the action. Other roles do not see a delete control.

6. Delete one test member you created only for this purpose.

You should see: that person disappears from Members and can no longer sign in. Do not delete a real tester you still need.

### What only you can do

Create staff logins, change roles, approve new players, delete a member, delete an activity line, and change Settings. Head Coach can edit members and post announcements, but those last items stay with you.

## Suggested order for one sitting

1. Admin prepares Settings, staff accounts, and today’s training.
2. Three players register, verify email, and wait.
3. Admin approves them and adds them to the training.
4. Players enroll a face, and one private or mixed player requests a private session and a belt.
5. The player checks in during the open window.
6. Coach confirms attendance, approves the belt, and prices the private session.
7. The private player accepts or declines, then uploads a receipt.
8. Head Coach edits a profile, reviews a face request, and posts an announcement.
9. Finance approves the receipt and checks Funds and Reports.
10. Admin checks the activity list, deletes one activity line, and confirms emails match on a coach’s user and member screens.

## Email checklist

| What happened | Who should get the email |
| --- | --- |
| New player registers | That player, with the verification link |
| Admin approves or declines the application | That player |
| Someone forgets a password | That account |
| A player is added to a training or an event | That player |
| A session’s date, time, or place changes | The people on it |
| An announcement is posted with email checked | The audience that was selected |
| A belt request is approved or declined | That player |
| A face change is approved or declined | That player |
| A private session is approved, given a new fee, cancelled, or the receipt is accepted or rejected | That player |
| A player declines a custom fee | The coach on that request |
| A player uploads a receipt | Finance and Admin |

If a club notice does not arrive, check spam, Settings, and that the member profile has the email. Verification and password reset still send when ordinary notices are switched off.
